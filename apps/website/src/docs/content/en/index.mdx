Welcome to the Papra documentation. Learn to organize your documents, run your own instance, and build integrations.

## What is Papra?

Papra is a document archiving platform that allows you to easily store and retrieve your documents. It is designed to be simple and easy to use, while also providing powerful features for managing your documents.
